Sounds like the real thing.

Vinylfy's advanced audio processing engine simulates every nuance of vinyl playback.

Real-time Processing

Vinylfy processes audio directly in your browser using WebAudio API for instant feedback. Simply upload your digital audio file and transform it within a few clicks. Preview your audio before downloading directly from your browser with playback controls.

All Formats

Vinylfy supports the major audio formats including MP3, WAV, FLAC, and AAC. Upload your digital audio as an MP3 and download it as an AAC file. The choice is yours.

Era-Specific Presets

Travel through time with presets tuned to replicate the specific sound signatures of the 1920s, 1950s, 1970s, and more. From 1920s Big Band to modern vinyl, Vinylfy's presets can make your digital audio sound like it was recorded on vinyl. For audiophiles who want total control, Vinylfy also offers a custom preset builder that allows you to dial in to your preferred sound.

Customize Your Sound

Vinylfy includes a built-in equalizer that allows you to fine-tune your audio to match your desired sound. From pops and crackles, to stereo separation, to frequency response, Vinylfy gives you the tools you need to get the sound you want.

Manage Metadata

Vinylfy allows you to manage metadata for your outputted audio files. You can add, edit, and remove metadata from your audio files. Want to give your "vinylfied" audio file a new name? Or add a new artist or album? Vinylfy has you covered. You can also add a cover art image to your audio file.

Works On Any Device

Vinylfy is a web app, so it works on any device with a web browser. PWA support allows you to install it on your device and use it like a native app.

Precision Control

Tweak every crackle.

For the audiophiles who need total control, dial in the exact amount of dust, wear, warp, and mechanical noise.

Community Driven

Open source at its core.

Vinylfy is open source at its core. We believe everyone should have access to enjoy a vinyl experience. Inspect the code, contribute features, or self-host your own version. Vinylfy is licensed under the AGPLv3 license.
